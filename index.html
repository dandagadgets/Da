Why we started

Karachi doesn't need another accessories stall.

Smartphones are essential — so the chargers, cases and earbuds that keep them running should be too. But too many shops sell accessories that fail in weeks, or charge premium prices for ordinary parts. Customers are left guessing.

D&A Gadgets is built to close that gap: genuine products, fair pricing, and a team that actually knows the difference between a real fast charger and a fake one.

Power, uninterrupted

Chargers and banks that don't cut corners.

Original fast chargers, wireless pads, and power banks up to 20,000 mAh — sourced from authorized distributors, checked for authenticity before they ever reach a shelf.

Sound & time

Earbuds and watches for daily life.

From wired basics to low-latency gaming earbuds, and smart watches that track sleep, heart rate and workouts — all backed by warranty on selected items.
